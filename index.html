The first offer is intentionally simple: we help define your sources, monitor them, and send briefings when something important appears or changes.

1. Choose sources

Tender portals, competitor pages, vendor docs, public announcements, hiring pages, or niche industry sites.

2. Define signals

New RFPs, pricing changes, product launches, grant windows, policy updates, or lead-quality signals.

3. Receive briefings

Daily or weekly summaries with source links, change notes, relevance, and suggested next action.

4. Give feedback

Mark alerts as useful or noisy so the watchlist and scoring improve for your business.

5. Productize what repeats

When a workflow proves valuable, we turn it into automation instead of guessing upfront.
